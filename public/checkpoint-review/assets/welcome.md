# Checkpoint Review: Do Approvers Catch Flawed Nominations?

**This is a demonstration study.** Every place, unit, and report is fictional. Please don't enter personal information.

## Your role

You are the **approving officer** at the engagement-approval checkpoint. A smart-intelligence (SI) targeting system will show you a series of target nominations. For each one, you will:

1. Review the nomination card, which has an oversight checklist, evidence panels, and expected effects.
2. Decide whether to **Approve**, **Return for more information**, or **Reject**.
3. Name your main concern, if you have one, and rate how confident you are.

Every nomination passes the standard oversight checklist and shows the system's confidence. You should still judge each one on its evidence.

The study takes about 8 to 10 minutes. Start with one practice trial.
